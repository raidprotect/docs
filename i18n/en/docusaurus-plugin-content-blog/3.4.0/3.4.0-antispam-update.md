Version 3.4.0 is **the big anti-spam update**: RaidProtect protects your server better and more precisely than ever. Also on the menu: **redesigned reports**, a **Transparency** system to showcase your moderation out in the open, and a command that **audits your server's security** in one click.

![RaidProtect Antispam Update (3.4.0) blog post social card](./assets/rp-antispam-update.webp)

<!--truncate-->

## 🛡️ A smarter anti-spam {#antispam}

The spammer flooding several channels at once? The hacked account dumping scam links? The [anti-spam](/docs/features/anti-spam) now handles them with far greater precision.

- **It spots spam spread across multiple channels** and applies a [firmer sanction](/docs/features/anti-spam#triggers) in that case.
- **It cleans up everything**: spam messages are deleted with every sanction, no exceptions.
- **You stay in control**: enable or turn off [each type of spam](/docs/features/anti-spam#triggers) independently, and choose the [sanction for scam images](/docs/features/anti-spam#sanctions).
- **New: attachment spam** can now be blocked too.

*(Premium)* You can also hide the public message announcing that a member has been sanctioned, for more discreet moderation.

---

## 🚩 Reports that finally make sense {#reports}

No more drowning in duplicate alerts. [Reports](/docs/features/reports) become a real tool for your moderation team.

- **One member, one alert**: all reports targeting the same person are [grouped together](/docs/features/reports#group), with a dedicated discussion thread to keep track of them.
- **Your members stay informed**: they can be [notified](/docs/features/reports#notify) when their report has been handled.
- **Your trusted members act fast** *(Premium)*: give them the power to apply a [preventive timeout](/docs/features/reports#trusted-role) while a moderator is on the way.
- **False reporters are neutralized**: those who abuse reports are [spotted and handled automatically](/docs/features/reports#bad-reporters).

---

## 📊 Show that you moderate {#transparency}

Nothing reassures your community like showing active, honest moderation. The new [Transparency](/docs/features/transparency) system is built for exactly that.

- **Automatic recaps**: [regularly](/docs/features/transparency#recaps) publish a summary of your server's moderation activity.
- **A public sanctions log**: display sanctions in a [dedicated channel](/docs/features/transparency#modlog), created in one click, choosing precisely what appears there (sanction type, source).
- **The recap on demand** with the [`/transparency`](/docs/features/transparency#command) command.

---

## 🔍 Secure your server in one click {#audit}

Is your server well protected? The new [`/audit`](/docs/features/audit) command gives it a **security score**, [points out the weak spots](/docs/features/audit#checks) (verification level, permissions, channels) and offers to [fix everything in one click](/docs/features/audit#fix).

---

## ✨ And also {#other}

- **Kick mode** *(Premium)*: during a raid, newcomers are kicked with an explanatory message and an invite to come back, rather than blocking every arrival. Need to let someone in? The [`/bypass raid`](/docs/features/raid-mode#bypass-raid) command is there for you.
- **RaidProtect replies to you** under the Discord security alerts caused by [DM Lock](/docs/features/dm-lock), to tell you what to do.
