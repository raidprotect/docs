Version 3.5.0 is **the Onboarding Update**: we rethought everything that happens when a member joins your server. On the menu: a **completely redone `/settings`**, a **welcome message**, a **next-generation captcha**, and a bot that now speaks **11 languages**.

![RaidProtect Onboarding Update (3.5.0) blog post social card](./assets/rp-onboarding-update.webp)

<!--truncate-->

## ⚙️ A brand-new /settings {#settings}

The heart of RaidProtect gets a makeover. The [`/settings`](/docs/setup#settings) command becomes a real dashboard, designed to find the right setting in a few clicks.

- **Five clear tabs**: General, Arrivals, Protection, Moderation and Utilities.
- **A completely redone interface** with Discord's new components (Components V2): more readable screens, and input windows for settings that need text.
- **A New badge** on new settings, so you miss nothing.
- **Premium settings stay visible**: you can browse everything, Premium is only required to change a reserved setting.

---

## 👋 Polished arrivals {#onboarding}

A member's first seconds on your server matter. The new [Arrivals and Welcome](/docs/features/onboarding) page gathers everything you need to welcome them well.

- **A welcome message** with a [welcome card, variables and buttons](/docs/features/onboarding#welcome-message). It is not sent during a raid, so the accounts of the raid are not welcomed.
- **Auto roles** that accept [several roles](/docs/features/onboarding#autorole), given once verification is passed.
- **The minimum account age** offers [several actions](/docs/features/onboarding#minage-action): kick the account that is too new, put it in jail until the required age, or block it on a captcha it can only solve once the age is reached.
- **Auto RaidMode chooses its response** during a raid: [block invites, kick newcomers, send them to the captcha, or make them wait at the captcha until the end of the raid](/docs/features/raid-mode#action). *(Kicking is reserved for Premium.)*

---

## 🧩 A next-generation captcha {#captcha}

The [captcha](/docs/features/captcha) is stronger against bots and more pleasant for your members.

- **Built-in verification**: in [Panel](/docs/features/captcha#display-mode) mode, the captcha [opens without leaving Discord](/docs/features/captcha#activity), in your server's colors, with a puzzle that is much harder for bots. If a result is doubtful, your team receives a report and decides whether or not to let the member in.
- **A trigger to your liking**: [on every arrival, or only during a raid](/docs/features/captcha#trigger).
- **A test mode** so your team can [redo the captcha](/docs/features/captcha#test-mode) without anything being applied.
- **A flawless configuration**: the captcha configuration flags the channels and roles that let verification be bypassed, and a button fixes them for you.

---

## 🛡️ For your team {#staff}

- **Who is on duty?** The new [`/onduty`](/docs/features/moderation#onduty) command and its on-duty role show your members which moderators are available.
- **Panels for the team** with [`/display staff`](/docs/features/display#staff), to publish in your staff channels: taking a role, going on duty, a reminder of useful commands.

---

## 🌍 RaidProtect speaks 11 languages {#languages}

Welcome to **Italian**, **Turkish**, **Vietnamese**, **Japanese** and **Chinese** (Simplified and Traditional)! By default, RaidProtect [follows your server's language](/docs/language#follow), with nothing to configure.

---

## ✨ And also {#other}

- **New [`/inviteinfo`](/docs/features/utilities#inviteinfo) command** to find out everything about an invite. It and `/userinfo` also install on your account, to use them everywhere on Discord.
- **Your data, your choices**: the new [`/privacy`](/docs/features/utilities#privacy) command, and a setting to enable [message caching](/docs/features/utilities#message-cache), needed to retrieve deleted messages with `/snipe`.
- **A time zone** for your server, used by the [transparency recaps](/docs/features/transparency#recaps).
- **[`/feedback`](/docs/features/utilities#feedback) offers a diagnosis** when you report an anti-spam problem.
