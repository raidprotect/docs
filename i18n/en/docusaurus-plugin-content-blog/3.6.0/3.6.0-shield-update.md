Version 3.6.0 is **the Shield Update**: we strengthened everything that protects your server day to day. On the menu: **DISCON**, a danger level you set in one click, an **Automod** that filters other servers and files, **bot arrivals under control**, and a new **setup wizard**.

![RaidProtect Shield Update (3.6.0) blog post social card](./assets/rp-shield-update.webp)

<!--truncate-->

## 🚨 DISCON, your alert level {#discon}

Modeled on DEFCON, [DISCON](/docs/features/discon) gives your server a **five-notch danger level**. You change it in one click, with `/discon` or from a [panel for the team](/docs/features/discon#panel), and RaidProtect hardens the server by itself at each level.

- **A graduated response**: verification, captcha required for every newcomer, DM Lock, raid mode, lockdown. The higher the level, the tighter the protections, and everything goes back to normal when it comes down.
- **A panel anywhere**: it is published in a text channel, or in the chat of a voice channel.
- **A status voice channel** (**Premium**) shows the current level, so the whole team sees it at a glance.

---

## 🧹 A more complete Automod {#automod}

- **Other servers**: the new [Other servers](/docs/features/automod#servers) filter checks invites, forwarded messages, channel or event links and tags from other servers. Block wild advertising without touching your partners, as a blocklist or an allowlist.
- **File extensions**: the [File extensions](/docs/features/automod#files) filter blocks groups of extensions (executables, archives, documents…) or allows only the ones you choose, server-wide or channel by channel. An `.exe` renamed to `.png` no longer gets through: we recognize disguised files.
- **ScamLens**: the [custom image catalog](/docs/features/scam-images#catalog) (**Premium**) blocks your own scam images, on top of our database.

---

## 🛡️ Anti-spam and HoneyPot {#antispam}

- **[Preventive slowmode](/docs/features/slowmode)** (**Premium**): the slowmode of your channels adjusts itself to hold the message rate you set, and goes back down when the channel calms.
- **"Spam" channels** can now be [monitored by the anti-spam](/docs/features/anti-spam#ignore), instead of always being ignored.
- **[HoneyPot](/docs/features/honeypot)**: a [delay before sanction](/docs/features/honeypot#grace) gives a good-faith member time to say they made a mistake, and both the ban and the jail can become permanent.

---

## 👋 Better-guarded arrivals {#arrivals}

- **An antibot**: give bots [their own roles](/docs/features/onboarding#bots), and automatically kick any bot added without your approval. The owner lets one in with [`/bypass bot`](/docs/features/onboarding#bypass-bot).
- **A smarter captcha**: the [Smart trigger](/docs/features/captcha#trigger) also checks newcomers whose account looks suspicious, outside of raids.
- **A captcha in your colors**: on a **Premium** server, your server's icon replaces the RaidProtect logo in the [verification](/docs/features/captcha#activity).
- **The minimum account age** now puts the account in [quarantine](/docs/features/onboarding#minage-action) until the required age, lifted automatically on the right date.

---

## ⚖️ For your moderation team {#moderation}

- **Synced quarantine**: the new Quarantine sanction follows [Discord's quarantine](/docs/features/sanctions#discord-quarantine), and each message blocked by Discord's AutoMod leaves a silent warning in the history.
- **[`/lock` on a category](/docs/features/channel-lock#lock-category)** locks all its channels at once, and the `silent` option of `/lock`, `/unlock` and `/slowmode` acts without announcing anything in the channel.
- **A default [message deletion](/docs/features/sanctions#ban-delete)** on ban, which you set once and for all.

---

## 🧭 A new setup wizard {#setup}

The new [`/setup` wizard](/docs/setup#wizard) guides you section by section, suggests the protections suited to your server and finishes with an audit preview.
