## 3.7.0 Identity Update <span className="badge-experiment">Experiment</span> {#3-7-0}

:::info Experiment
The Identity Update is an experiment: until its release in 3.7.0, it is in public beta for [Premium](/en/premium) servers. Its content may still change.
:::

- New [`/alt`](./features/alt-accounts.md#alt) command and [alt accounts](./features/alt-accounts.md): link the accounts of a single person, choose their main account, and their sanctions (ban, kick, mute, jail, timeout, softban) extend to the linked accounts. Removing the sanction of one removes it for all. <SuggestionLink id="404" /> <SuggestionLink id="263" />
- [Linked account sanctions](./features/alt-accounts.md#modes) can be set per type, with a **Trap** mode and an [evasion sanction](./features/alt-accounts.md#evasion) for the linked account that arrives or writes during the sanction.
- Linked accounts that are absent from the server are sanctioned when they arrive.
- A sanction extended to the group becomes a [group sanction](./features/alt-accounts.md#group-sanction) in `/sanctions` and `/modview`: you cancel, delete or edit it at once for all the accounts.
- Sanction logs mention the linked accounts, with a "**Linked accounts**" button to the `/alt` panel in embed format.
- New [One account per person](./features/alt-accounts.md#single-account) setting: secondary accounts are put in quarantine.
- The [captcha](./features/captcha.md#activity-alts) spots alt accounts: it links accounts it is sure are the same person by itself, and suggests probable links to your team.
- [Linked account sanctions](./features/alt-accounts.md#modes) are reserved for **Premium**. Without Premium, `/alt` links and views accounts with no limit, and One account per person remains available.

## 3.6.0 Shield Update {#3-6-0}

- New **[DISCON](./features/discon.md)**: a five-notch server danger level, adjustable in one click with the `/discon` command or from a [panel for the team](./features/discon.md#panel). Each level automatically hardens the server (verification, captcha, DM Lock, raid mode, lockdown), and a [voice channel](./features/discon.md#voice) shows the current level.
- [Anti-spam](./features/anti-spam.mdx) V3, already in place for everyone since 3.5.0:
  - New detection engine: near-duplicates, repeated links, emojis and gibberish, flood, and spam spread across several channels.
  - [Presets](./features/anti-spam.mdx#presets) and sanctions per family, with an [advanced mode](./features/anti-spam.mdx#advanced) to set each trigger and its [duration](./features/anti-spam.mdx#duration).
- [Anti-spam](./features/anti-spam.mdx#ignore): new option to monitor channels whose name contains “spam”, which were always ignored until now. <SuggestionLink id="600" />
- New [preventive slowmode](./features/slowmode.md) (**Premium**): the slowmode of the chosen channels adjusts itself to hold the message rate you set.
- New [Other servers](./features/automod.md#servers) filter in [Automod](./features/automod.md): invites, forwarded messages, channel or event links and tags from other servers, as a blocklist or an allowlist.
- New [File extensions](./features/automod.md#files) filter in Automod: block groups of extensions or allow only some, server-wide or channel by channel, even when a file is disguised under another extension. **Premium** servers get more per-channel rules.
- [HoneyPot](./features/honeypot.md): new [delay before sanction](./features/honeypot.md#grace), which gives a good-faith member time to say they made a mistake.
- [HoneyPot](./features/honeypot.md#sanction): ban and jail can be permanent.
- New [Smart trigger](./features/captcha.md#trigger) for the captcha: besides raids, it checks newcomers whose account looks suspicious.
- New **Quarantine** sanction, synchronized with [Discord's quarantine](./features/sanctions.mdx#discord-quarantine).
- [ScamLens](./features/scam-images.md): [image catalog](./features/scam-images.md#catalog) (**Premium**) to block your own scam images.
- Each message blocked by Discord's AutoMod creates a silent [warning](./features/sanctions.mdx) in the name of "Discord Automod".
- New [setup wizard](./setup.md#wizard): a guided path section by section, protections suggested for your server and an audit preview to finish.
- [`/auth`](./features/authentication-manager.mdx#auth) assigns several protected roles at once, in a single command. <SuggestionLink id="601" />
- Authentication Manager [separate permissions role](./features/authentication-manager.mdx#strategies): channel permissions of the original role are moved to the 🔑 role, and a checkbox lets you give administrators view access to every channel.
- [`/auth`](./features/authentication-manager.mdx#logs-tab): sessions are logged in the Authentication Manager log channel.
- [`/lock` and `/unlock`](./features/channel-lock.md#lock) lock and unlock a whole category, and its channels stay synced. <SuggestionLink id="595" />
- New [message deletion on ban](./features/sanctions.mdx#ban-delete) setting: the history deleted by default with `/ban`, `?ban` and `/tempban`. <SuggestionLink id="592" />
- [`/snipe`](./features/moderation.mdx#snipe) warns you when message caching is disabled and offers to enable it. Using it turns message caching on.
- [`/lock`, `/unlock`](./features/channel-lock.md#lock) and [`/slowmode`](./features/moderation.mdx#slowmode): new `silent` option to act without an announcement in the channel.
- [DISCON](./features/discon.md#panel-channel): the panel can be posted in the chat of a voice channel.
- [Captcha Activity](./features/captcha.md#activity): on a **Premium** server, the server icon replaces the RaidProtect logo.
- [Authentication Manager](./features/authentication-manager.mdx#manage-methods): you can rename a passkey.
- New [Bot arrival](./features/onboarding.md#bots) section: roles given automatically to bots that join the server, and kicking of unauthorized bots, with [`/bypass bot`](./features/onboarding.md#bypass-bot) to let one in.
- [Welcome message](./features/onboarding.md#welcome-buttons): each button can have its own emoji.
- [Minimum account age](./features/onboarding.md#minage-action): jail becomes a quarantine, lifted automatically when the account reaches the required age. Ongoing quarantines adjust when you change the minimum age: lifted if it goes down or is disabled, extended if it goes up.
- [`/unjail`](./features/moderation.mdx#unjail) refuses to lift a quarantine (minimum account age, Discord) and explains how to do it. The end of a jail keeps the role of a quarantined member.
- Several new logs.
- Fixes:
  - `/unlock` restores exactly the permissions from before the lock, and RaidProtect no longer loses its own access.
  - `/auth` reports a disabled role instead of opening a fake session.
  - A member sent to [jail](./features/moderation.mdx#jail) is disconnected from voice.
  - The unverified role of the [captcha](./features/captcha.md) can no longer be chosen as an auto role.

## 3.5.0 Onboarding Update (10/19/2026) {#3-5-0}

- Rework of **[`/settings`](./setup.md#settings)**: a five-tab dashboard (General, Arrivals, Protection, Moderation, Utilities), with Discord's new interface (Components V2) and input windows for settings that need text. A **New** badge marks new settings. [Premium](/en/premium) settings stay visible: Premium is only required to change them. <SuggestionLink id="583" />
- Arrivals:
  - New [welcome message](./features/onboarding.md#welcome-message), with a welcome card in the member's name, variables and buttons. It is not sent while raid mode or the Join Lock is active, so the accounts of a raid are not welcomed. <SuggestionLink id="110" />
  - [Auto roles](./features/onboarding.md#autorole) now have their own setting, separate from the captcha one, and accept several roles. They are given once verification is passed. <SuggestionLink id="586" /> <SuggestionLink id="166" />
  - The [minimum account age](./features/onboarding.md#minage) offers [several actions](./features/onboarding.md#minage-action): kick the account that is too new, put it in jail until it reaches the required age, or hold it on a captcha it can only solve once it reaches that age. <SuggestionLink id="555" />
  - You choose how [Auto RaidMode](./features/raid-mode.md#action) reacts during a raid: block invites, kick newcomers (**Premium**), send them to the captcha, or make them wait at the captcha until the raid is over. Only invite blocking still requires Community to be enabled on the server.
  - The [captcha](./features/captcha.md) gains a Panel [display mode](./features/captcha.md#display-mode) and an Automatic or Always [trigger](./features/captcha.md#trigger). Its configuration flags the channels and roles that let verification be bypassed, with a button to fix them.
  - In Panel mode, the captcha [opens without leaving Discord](./features/captcha.md#activity): a harder puzzle for bots, in your server's colors. If a result is doubtful, your team receives a report and decides whether to let the member in.
  - New [test mode](./features/captcha.md#test-mode): your team can take the captcha to try it out, without any action being applied.
- Protection:
  - The [Authentification Manager](./features/authentication-manager.mdx) is now set up in `/settings`, and its authentication pages are translated.
  - The [DM Lock](./features/dm-lock.mdx) is set in an input window.
- Moderation:
  - New [`/onduty`](./features/moderation.mdx#onduty) command and on-duty role to flag available moderators.
  - New [`/display staff`](./features/display.mdx#staff) panels to post in your staff channels: role pickup, going on duty, reminder of useful commands.
  - `/discord-sanctions` displays global statistics when no user is specified.
- Utilities:
  - New [`/inviteinfo`](./features/utilities.mdx#inviteinfo) command. `/userinfo` and `/inviteinfo` also install on your account, to use them everywhere.
  - New [`/privacy`](./features/utilities.mdx#privacy) command, and new **Message caching** setting, required for [`/snipe`](./guides/malfunctions.md#snipe) to find deleted messages.
  - [`/feedback`](./features/utilities.mdx#feedback) offers a diagnosis when you report an anti-spam problem.
- General:
  - RaidProtect now speaks [11 languages](./language.md) with the arrival of Italian, Turkish, Vietnamese, Japanese and Simplified and Traditional Chinese. By default, it [follows the server's language](./language.md#follow). <SuggestionLink id="575" /> <SuggestionLink id="585" />
  - New server [time zone](./setup.md#timezone), used by transparency recaps.
  - [Message commands](./guides/prefix.md) and their [permissions](./guides/prefix.md#permissions) are set in two separate screens. Command permissions set in Discord (Integrations) also apply to sanctions from the "Sanction User" menu (right-click on a member).
  - [Bot customization](./setup.md#customize) (**Premium**) is now set in `/settings`.
- Various fixes and translation fixes.

## 3.4.0 Antispam Update (07/18/2026) {#3-4-0}

- [Anti-spam](./features/anti-spam.mdx) V2:
  - **Multi-channel spam**: a dedicated sanction when the spam spans several channels (Jail by default, Timeout minimum).
  - New **attachment spam** [trigger](./features/anti-spam.mdx#triggers) (disabled by default).
  - [Simulation](./features/anti-spam.mdx#simulation) to understand why a spam was sanctioned or not.
  - Spam messages are now **deleted whatever the sanction** (ban, jail, mute, kick or timeout).
  - The **anti-scam sanction is configurable** from the [anti-spam sanctions menu](./features/anti-spam.mdx#triggers) and follows the [HoneyPot sanction](./features/honeypot.md#sanction) by default; anti-scam incidents are sanctioned and logged instantly. <SuggestionLink id="573" />
  - Each trigger can be disabled individually with the new **Disable** option, and the sensitivity levels are replaced by a simple toggle.
  - **Mute** and **Jail** fall back to a capped timeout when the corresponding role is not configured.
  - Selfbots sending rich embeds are flagged, and scams blocked by ScamLens are counted in the [HoneyPot](./features/honeypot.md) flagged-accounts counter.
- Complete rework of the **[Reports](./features/reports.md)** system: <SuggestionLink id="503" />
  - Reports are now grouped into **incidents**: several reports about the same member fold into a single incident, with a [configurable grouping mode](./features/reports.md#group).
  - The report commands now open a **reason modal**, with a "what are you reporting" checklist on profile reports (including voice and DM activity).
  - Each incident now opens a **thread** under the report: profile card of the reported member, per-message reason headers, name of the resolving moderator and rejection notes.
  - New [reporter notification](./features/reports.md#notify): reporters can be notified in DM when their report is handled, with a configurable detail level (up to the accepted/rejected outcome). <SuggestionLink id="550" />
  - New [trusted role](./features/reports.md#trusted-role) (**Premium**): trusted members can apply a **preventive 24h timeout** when reporting, and their urgent reports ping the moderation role again.
  - New [bad reporters protection](./features/reports.md#bad-reporters): repeat false reporters can be flagged, blocked or sanctioned automatically (configurable threshold, window and mode).
  - Open report incidents are automatically closed when the member is sanctioned.
  - Preventive sanctions are rewritten when a report is accepted, and canceled when it is rejected.
- New **[Transparency](./features/transparency.md)** system:
  - [Periodic recaps](./features/transparency.md#recaps) of the server's moderation activity, posted automatically on a configurable schedule.
  - New [public modlog](./features/transparency.md#modlog): sanctions can be published in a public channel, filtered by type and source, with a one-click creation of a read-only #public-modlog channel.
  - New [`/transparency` command](./features/transparency.md#command) to show the recap of the last closed period on demand.
- New [`/audit` command](./features/audit.md): a full **security audit** of the server with an overall score, [detailed checks](./features/audit.md#checks) (verification level, permissions, channels) and [one-click fixes](./features/audit.md#fix). Admin roles can be integrated into the Authentification Manager directly from the audit.
- New [kick mode](./features/raid-mode.md#kick-mode) (**Premium**) for raid mode and Join Lock: newcomers are kicked with an explanation DM and a rejoin invite button, instead of natively pausing invites. The new [`/bypass raid` command](./features/raid-mode.md#bypass-raid) lets specific users in while it is active. <SuggestionLink id="144" />
- [Message commands](./guides/prefix.md#permissions) can now follow the **Discord command permissions** (Integrations tab) via a new option in `/settings`. <SuggestionLink id="369" />
- RaidProtect now replies under **Discord's security incident alerts** when they are caused by the [DM Lock](./features/dm-lock.mdx), quoting the alert and explaining where to adjust the setting.
- The [minimum account age](./features/raid-mode.md#minage) kick log now shows the required threshold as a timestamp.
- Settings and setup:
  - The `/settings` main menu is reorganized into logical rows.
  - The setup message now links the commands directly and offers a button to change the logs channel.
  - A **disable captcha** button is offered when the captcha channel was deleted.
- Internal migration of the Discord layer to shared libraries (`@fca.gg/discord`) to speed up future updates.
- Fixes:
  - Clearer error messages distinguishing **timeout** from **mute**, and administrator targets from role hierarchy issues.
  - Fixed the automatic conversion of Discord AutoMod timeouts.
  - Fixed `/serverinfo` vanity URL access, rules and statistics display.
  - Fixed the `/discord-sanctions` alias and message-command prefix usage.
  - Fixed the Jail applied to administrators with the replace-roles mode.
  - Hardened webhook URL validation.
  - Various fixes: sanction logs, captcha, DM Lock messages, moderation notes, Premium detection.
  - Various translation fixes.
