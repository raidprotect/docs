The `/display` command allows you to create and manage information panels published by RaidProtect on your server. It has two parts:

- `/display public`: panels for your members (DM Lock, sanctions, reports...).
- `/display staff`: panels reserved for your team ([DISCON](./discon.md), role taking, documentation, going on duty).

Use the command: ```/display public``` or ```/display staff```

:::info
Both subcommands require the "**Manage Server**" permission.
:::

## ❓ How it works {#working}

Each information panel is published by the bot in a channel of your choice. You have multiple slots available to create independent panels, each with its own content and features.

:::info
**Public panel limits:** 2 slots in the free version, 4 in the [premium](/en/premium) version, plus 1 slot reserved for Jail content. The [captcha](./captcha.md) panel does not use up a slot.
:::

## 📄 Content types {#content}

Each public panel can contain one or more of the following content types:

- **DM Lock**: Informs members about the [DM Lock](./dm-lock.mdx) status. Displays whether DMs are locked or not, and explains that only friends, staff, bots and previous contacts can send DMs.
- **Jail**: Displays a message intended for jailed members, indicating that their access to the server has been restricted due to a rule violation.
- **Sanctions**: Tells members how to view their personal sanctions history via the `/my-sanctions` command.
- **Report**: Explains to members how to report a user or a message via the `/report` command or the context menu (right-click).

## ⚙️ Features {#features}

In addition to content, each public panel can enable interactive features:

- **Translation**: Adds a button allowing members to translate the panel into any language supported by Discord.
- **Sanctions access**: Adds a "**View my sanctions**" button allowing members to directly view their sanctions.
- **Report**: Adds a "**Report a member**" button to quickly report a user.

## 📌 Reserved slots {#reserved-slot}

Some panels are managed by their module, not by `/display`:

- **Jail**: the last public slot is reserved for Jail content. Its channel is only set in `/settings`, "**Moderation**" tab, "**Sanctions**", then [Jail](./sanctions.mdx#jail) ("**Edit channel**"). You can, however, add content and features to it from `/display public`.
- **Captcha**: the [captcha](./captcha.md) panel in Panel mode is posted and removed by the captcha itself (`/settings`, "**Arrivals**" tab, "**Captcha**"). It can neither be created nor deleted from `/display`.
- **DISCON**: the [DISCON](./discon.md#panel) panel occupies the reserved slot of `/display staff`. Its channel is only set with `/discon`.

## 🛡️ Staff panels {#staff}

The `/display staff` command manages panels intended for your team, to be published in staff-only channels.

Use the command: ```/display staff```

:::info
**Staff panel limits:** 1 slot in the free version, 4 in the [premium](/en/premium) version, plus 1 slot reserved for the [DISCON](./discon.md) panel.
:::

### Staff-only content {#staff-content}

Each staff content automatically adds its button to the panel:

- **DISCON**: the server's danger level and its five buttons. This content is reserved for the DISCON slot, managed from the [DISCON configuration](./discon.md#config). You can add other content to this panel from `/display staff`.
- **Take a role**: a button that opens the choice of roles protected by the [Authentification Manager](./authentication-manager.mdx#auth), like the `/auth` command.
- **Documentation**: a button to the moderation documentation, and a reminder of useful commands (`/modview`, `/sanctions`, `/clear`).
- **On duty**: a button that adds or removes the [on-duty role](./moderation.mdx#onduty), like the `/onduty` command.

The **Translation** feature is also available on staff panels.

:::warning
Staff panel buttons do not check the permissions of whoever clicks: **access to the channel acts as the control**. Publish them only in channels reserved for your team.
:::

## 🔄 Updating panels {#update}

When you change the bot's language or the panel content is updated by RaidProtect, an indicator flags the panels that are out of date. You can then click the "**Update**" button to refresh the panel.

## ✏️ Edit or delete a panel {#edit}

From the `/display public` or `/display staff` menu, each occupied slot has buttons to:

- **Edit**: Change the channel, content or features of the panel.
- **Delete**: Remove the panel from the channel and free up the slot.

The channel of reserved slots (Jail, DISCON) is changed from their module.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

### What is the difference between `/display public` and `/display staff`?

`/display public` publishes panels for your members (DMs, sanctions, reports). `/display staff` publishes panels for your team: DISCON level, taking a protected role, documentation and going on duty.

### How many panels can I create?

Public: 2 panels in the free version, 4 in premium, plus the Jail slot. Staff: 1 panel in the free version, 4 in premium, plus the DISCON slot.

### Why can't I delete the captcha or DISCON panel?

These panels belong to their module: the captcha is managed in `/settings` and DISCON with `/discon`. Deleting them from `/display` would break the feature without warning.
